Microsoft Intune device compliance failure investigation cheat sheet

Trace a noncompliant device through check-in, assigned policies and individual setting results.

Microsoft Intune admin center; access, reporting and policy details depend on roles, licensing and platform.

Walkthrough

These are portal procedures. Review the affected device using authorized access without disabling Conditional Access or changing policies during evidence collection.

Step 1: Find the device and user in Intune

Identify the affected device

Confirm the device identity, operating system and owner before comparing policy results. Duplicate or stale device records can lead to the wrong investigation.

Step 2: Inspect the device last check-in

Review check-in time

Compare the last check-in with the incident and report freshness. A device that cannot check in may not have reported its current policy state.

Step 3: Open the device compliance policy results

Inspect assigned compliance policies

Identify the applicable policies and their reported statuses. Pending, noncompliant and error statuses have different meanings and require different follow-up.

Step 4: Open the relevant policy and setting details

Inspect individual settings

Record the specific setting and reported reason for noncompliance. Protect device and user details when sharing the evidence with other administrators.

Step 5: Review the related Entra sign-in event

Compare Conditional Access evidence

Compare the device and compliance context recorded for the failed sign-in. A noncompliant policy result alone does not prove which access policy blocked a particular event.

Situation

A user reports that access is blocked after a device compliance warning.

Investigation sequence

1. Find the device and user in Intune

Identify the affected device

Confirm the correct device record and affected user before investigating.

2. Inspect the device last check-in

Review check-in time

Record the latest check-in and policy report timestamps.

3. Open the device compliance policy results

Inspect assigned compliance policies

Inspect assigned policy results and the exact failing setting.

4. Open the relevant policy and setting details

Inspect individual settings

Compare the device context with the related Entra sign-in event.

Example findings

Illustrative findings: the device has checked in recently and reports that its encryption requirement is not satisfied.

Interpret the findings

The specific policy result directs the device team to the encryption control; the sign-in log is needed to establish how access was evaluated.

Verify the outcome

After the device owner resolves the confirmed issue, inspect a fresh compliance report and sign-in event.

Escalation handoff

Share sanitized device identifier, policy name, setting result and event time with the device and identity teams.
